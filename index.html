03 · Life of a deal

A typical transaction, start to finish.

Illustrative only. Every deal differs.7

  1. 1

    Contract signed

    An investor has a property under contract and needs short-term closing capital.

  2. 2

    Underwriting

    The servicer reviews the deal, the parties, the contracts and the exit.

  3. 3

    Title verified

    Title company, closing agent and wire instructions are checked before any wire.

  4. 4

    Funds to escrow

    Capital is wired to the title company for that named closing.

  5. 5

    Closing & repayment

    The deal closes and the advance is repaid from closing proceeds.

  6. 6

    Reported to you

    The quarter's activity is summarized in the next letter and statement.

Steps 3 to 5 run through the title company's escrow
